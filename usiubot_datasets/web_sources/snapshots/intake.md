### What Makes USIU-Africa Different?

#### 1. Unique Programs

Our academic programs are enriched by our international culture, U.S. Kenya accreditation, global teaching standards.

#### 2. World-Class Facilities

Exciting learning experiences combined with top-of-the-range facilities provide a valuable global education.

#### 3. Affordable

Student receive more than market value in return for fees. Our policy ensures students focus on learning.

#### 4. Exchange Programs

Study in five continents. Become a global citizen through exposure in classes, clubs, and sports.

#### 5. Industry Partnerships

Interact with global organizations. Industry partners provide guest lectures and employment opportunities.

#### 6. Cultural Diversity

More than sixty nationalities. Interaction produces graduates comfortable changing every society.

#### 7. IT Facilities

Best-in-class e-learning platforms. Create innovative solutions to global challenges.

#### 8. Library Facilities

24/7 access to electronic resources. Recommended reading material provided every semester.

#### 9. Co-curricular Activities

Develop holistic skills through extensively-funded club, sports and student government.

#### 10. Student/Faculty Ratio

Personalized attention produces self-inspired high achievers ready for successful careers.

#### 11. Governance Representation

Feedback is incorporated into policy. Students are encouraged to develop and demonstrate the ability to think critically and creatively.

## Guide To

## Application

##### 1

##### Apply online

Download and fill in the appropriate [application forms](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/intake/#application-forms), then follow the steps 2 -5 on the right.

##### 2

##### Payments

New applicants must pay a non-refundable application fee of KES 3,500 for undergraduates and KES 4000 for postgraduates. Kindly visit [this page](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/admission-fees/fee-payment-refunds/) for payment details.

##### 3

##### Scan documents

Scan all academic certificates, payment receipt, and the form. Send them to [admit@usiu.ac.ke](mailto:admit@usiu.ac.ke).

##### 4

##### Processing

Documents are processed within 2-4 working days. Conditional acceptance is granted first; full admission follows verification.
