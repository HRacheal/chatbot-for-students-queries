# How to Apply

The application form saves your progress, so don't worry about completing it in one sitting!

We in the Admissions Office and USIU-Africa as a whole believe that you have made the right choice and are very pleased to have you with us.

Your education takes place in the campus environment, as well as the classrooms. We are committed to provide services that benefit your health, safely, and welfare. We are equally committed to enhancing the opportunities for learning through various activities, recreational programs and personal relationships, which make up the student life.

## Apply

#### 01

#### Confirm Course Information

We encourage you to explore all our available programs on [**this page**](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/study/our-programs/). Once you have identified a course of interest, please visit its dedicated page to review all details. Keep in mind that entry requirements and intake dates vary by course, so it's important to confirm the specific information for your selection.

#### 02

#### Confirmation of Admission Requirements

Secondly, you are strongly encouraged to visit the **[Admission Requirements](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/admission-requirements/)** page to familiarize yourself with all the necessary information and documents required for your application.

#### 03

#### Filling The Application Form

You're now halfway there—let's get you one step closer to joining us! Please scroll to the bottom of this page where you will find your respective application forms. Kindy download them, fill them then scan and send them to **[admit@usiu.ac.ke](mailto:admit@usiu.ac.ke)** (clearly stating that you are applying for a program at USIU-Africa) or drop them manually to the admissions office on campus. Do not forget to include your academic documents together with any other required supporting documents.

Please note that some programs require different application forms, so make sure you select and complete the correct one for your course of choice. Completing your application is an important step toward beginning your academic journey with us!

Before applying, kindly ensure you have all the required admission documents and supporting materials ready. To help you prepare, use the checklists provided on application forms.

#### 04

#### Application Fee Payment

New applicants must pay a non-refundable application fee of KES 3,500 for undergraduate applicants and KES 4000 for postgraduate applicants. Kindly visit [this page](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/admission-fees/fee-payment-refunds/) for payment details. Make sure to attach your proof of payment when making the application.

#### 05

#### Application Review

The admissions office will review your application to confirm you meet our requirements. If so, communication will be sent to you via email, together with the fee structure, brochure and any other relevant documents that might be required for admission.
