# CBET Realignment

Competency-Based Education and Training (CBET) is the university-level continuation of Kenya’s Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC/BECF) reform.

### What is CBET, and why it matters now

Competency-Based Education and Training (CBET) is the university-level continuation of Kenya’s Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC/BECF) reform. Learners now reaching Grade 12 are trained around seven core competencies and enter university through defined pathways (STEM; Social Sciences; Arts & Sports Science) rather than the old subject-cluster system.

The Commission for University Education (CUE) has directed all accredited universities to review and realign their undergraduate programmes to the CBET framework, with full submission due by July 2027. USIU-Africa is proactively preparing now so the transition strengthens, rather than disrupts, the quality of every degree we offer.

- _“CBET is not a replacement for our academic standards; it is a sharper way of demonstrating them, tied to clearly defined, industry-relevant competencies.”_
- _“USIU-Africa is preparing early and systematically, so no programme is rushed and no student is disadvantaged.”_

### Timeline at a Glance

The rollout runs in seven stages from mid-2026 to mid-2027, moving from sensitization through faculty training, curriculum redesign, multi-level approval, and final submission to CUE.

| Milestone | Timeline | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| Sensitization of University Community | Jul–Aug 2026 | Town-halls, awareness campaign, FAQs & briefs for staff, students, management |
| Training of Faculty and Staff | Aug–Sep 2026 | Retooling on CBET pedagogy, outcomes-based assessment, competence mapping |
| Curriculum Needs Assessment & Gap Analysis | Sep–Oct 2026 | Review current curricula against CBET/CUE/KNQF; industry consultations |
| Realignment of Curricula | Oct–Dec 2026 | Redesign programmes, courses & outcomes; validate with industry |
| Internal Approvals | Jan–May 2027 | School Academic Committees → EE Committee → University Senate |
| University Council Approval | Jun/Jul 2027 | Final institutional sign-off |
| Finalization & Submission to CUE | Jul-27 | Package and submit all reviewed undergraduate programmes |
