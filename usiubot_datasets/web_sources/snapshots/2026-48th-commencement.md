  ### Security Advisory

  To ensure a safe, secure, and enjoyable experience for all graduands and participants, everyone is requested to observe the following security guidelines:

  ### 1. Access Control and Parking Management

  Heavy traffic is expected on roads leading to the University. Please arrive early to allow sufficient time for security screening and parking.

  - Graduands walking to the University before 7:00 a.m. will access the University through the Main Gate (Gate A); thereafter, graduands will access campus through Gate B, Gate C and Gate D, which are accessible via Mirema Road/Drive, Lumumba Drive, and Northern Bypass Road.
  - All graduands are required to be in their graduation gowns while accessing the University.
  - Motorists are advised to use the following Google Maps coordinates for gates' directions:
    - Gate A, (Main Gate): QVJH+7M3 (-1.219362, 36.87917)
    - Gate B: QVPH+CXR (-1.213888, 36.87989)
    - Gate C: QVRJ+M26 (-1.208337, 36.88002)
    - Gate D: QVRJ+8RM (-1.209162, 36.88205)
  - RED-RED e-Ticket, RED-ORANGE e-Ticket and BLUE e-Tickets holders will access the University through the Main Gate (Gate A).
  - Upon scanning their e-Tickets, Ticketsasa team will issue guests with a corresponding RED, ORANGE or BLUE wristband, depending on the e-Ticket category.
  - RED e-Tickets/ RED and ORANGE wristband holders: Drop-off will be at the Parking Lot A-exit point, and their vehicles will be parked in Parking Lot A.
  - BLUE e-Tickets/wrist band holders: Drop-off will be at the Parking Lot C-exit point or the Auditorium roundabout, and their vehicles will be parked in Parking Lot C (Student Center).
  - Faculty and Staff are required to use their valid University ID cards for access, and they will access the University through the Main gate (Gate A). They will park their vehicles at the School of Science and Technology (SST), School of Humanities & Social Sciences (SHSS).
  - Parking Lot D (adjacent to the Auditorium/Library) and Parking Lot B will be reserved for First Aid Station/Ambulance, and the graduands' line-up/subsequent procession to the Graduation Square respectively.
  - All other motorists and invited guests with Yellow e-Tickets will access the University through Gates B, C, and D. They will park in the designated open field behind SHSS.
  - Please note that Electronic Access Tickets are barcoded, and will be for a single use.
  - Persons with disability displaying valid disability stickers on their vehicles will access the campus through the Main Gate (Gate A) at all times. They will park in Library Parking Space, adjacent to the generator room.
  - Ushers and security personnel will be available to direct visitors to the appropriate entry and parking areas.
  - Motorists are not permitted to park vehicles on the soccer field.
  - No vehicles shall remain parked on campus overnight on Friday, 25 September 2026. Vehicles left on campus overnight will be towed to the nearest police station at the owner's expense.
  - All motorists must observe traffic laws and maintain a maximum speed limit of 10 KPH while on campus.
  - Ensure that your vehicle is securely locked and that all windows are fully closed before leaving it unattended.
  - Do not leave valuables visible inside your vehicle.
  - All motorists are expected to exit the University through the same gate they used to enter.

  To access the campus using any of the 4 available gates, key in the following Geo-Coordinates into your Google Maps for direction:

  - Gate 'A' (Main Gate) – QVJH+7M3 (-1.219362, 36.87917)
  - Gate 'B' – QVPH+CXR (-1.213888, 36.87989)
  - Gate 'C' – QVRJ+M26 (-1.208337, 36.88002)
  - Gate 'D' – QVRJ+8RM (-1.209162, 36.88205)

  ### 2. Security Coverage

  - Security will be intensified across the campus and along key roads leading to the University. A contingent of **armed, traffic, uniformed, and undercover police officers** will complement the University's security arrangements.

  ### 3. Photography and Videography

  - Only the University's designated official video/photography will be allowed.

  ### 4. Personal Belongings and Security Measures

  - Keep your valuables with you at all times.
  - Where necessary, entrust your belongings only to someone you know and trust.
  - **Security screening:** All visitors, their vehicles, and personal belongings will be subject to security screening and inspection.
  - **Prohibited items:** The following items are prohibited on campus: psychotropic substances, bottles, cans, alcoholic drinks, confetti, outside food or beverages, weapons including firearms, knives, and animals.

  ### 5. On-Site Safety

  - **Hydration:** Water will be available throughout the venue. Please do not bring water or other beverages from outside.
  - **Situational awareness:** Be mindful of your surroundings, and report any safety or security concerns immediately to security officers or event staff.
  - **Cooperation:** Follow the instructions of ushers, security personnel, and other authorized event personnel at all times to help maintain order and ensure everyone's safety.

  ### 6. General Information

  - **Stay for the full ceremony:** all graduands are requested to remain seated until the ceremony concludes.
  - **Hawking:** There will be **NO hawking or unauthorized commercial activities** within the University.
  - **Taxis:** No taxis will be allowed onto the campus. Visitors using taxis will be dropped off outside the designated gates and access the venue on foot.

  ### 7. Lost & Found Items

  - All lost and found items will be forwarded/placed at the CSOB security desk—owners will claim them after proper identification/verification.

  ### 8. Emergency Services & Procedures:

  - A First Aid station staffed by medical personnel will be available to attend to urgent medical needs.
  - In the event of an emergency, remain calm and follow all announcements and instructions issued by event staff and security personnel.

  ### 9. Emergency Contacts

  ### Lifelong Rewards and Benefits

  1. Financial Aid for continuing education opportunities

  **a) Family Tuition Waiver**
  This is 10% tuition waiver in recognition of parents/guardians or sponsors continuous support for USIU-Africa. Eligible applicant must have two nuclear family (siblings) members previously or currently enrolled at USIU-Africa. The Applicant must maintain a cumulative GPA of 2.0 at Undergraduate level and a cumulative GPA of 3.0 at graduate level. Doctoral program students are eligible from the Fall 2025 Semester.

  **b) Legatee Tuition Waiver**
  This is a 10% tuition waiver for children of members of the USIU-Africa Alumni Association. This waiver may be availed to eligible applicants upon admissions and is subject to availability of the allocated funds. The Applicants must maintain a cumulative GPA of 2.0 at undergraduate level and a cumulative GPA of 3.0 at graduate level. Doctoral program students are eligible from Fall 2025.

  **c) Graduate Waiver**
  This is a 10% tuition waiver for USIU-Africa alumni who are pursuing their graduate studies at the university. Beneficiaries of the grant must maintain a cumulative GPA of 3.2 and above for the continued eligibility and are not legible for the Family Tuition waiver or the Legatee Tuition Waiver. Doctoral program students are eligible from Fall 2025.

  2. Alumni library services
