# Housing & Accommodation

Explore our several accommodation options.

We have an objective to provide quality housing services and programs that enhance residential student success in a clean, safe and inclusive living-learning environment – a “_home away from home_”.

### On Campus Housing

The student halls of residence allow them to live close to instructional and academic support buildings, the dining facility and other university services. Two residential buildings accommodate 258 students on campus. Facilities in the buildings include the Table Tennis/Darts/Pool Room, three TV Rooms and a Study Room.

![](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Hostel.jpg)

### Facilities

At USIU-Africa, our residence halls are designed to provide students with a safe, comfortable, and supportive living environment. Each fully furnished room accommodates two students and comes equipped with beds, mattresses, study tables, chairs, and wardrobes. Communal bathrooms are available on every floor, shared by residents of four rooms, while telephones are provided on each floor for incoming calls.

To ensure a positive student experience, Resident Assistants live on every wing, offering guidance, support, and fostering a vibrant community atmosphere.

Because on-campus housing is limited, priority is given to international students. If space allows, students from outside Nairobi are then considered. To qualify, you must be enrolled as a full-time student taking at least 9 credit units (or a minimum of 6 units for graduate students).

### Room Reservation

USIU-Africa residence halls provide fully furnished rooms designed to accommodate two students. Each room is equipped with two beds and mattresses, study tables, chairs, bookshelves, and wardrobes. Communal bathrooms are located on each floor and are shared by occupants from four rooms, while a telephone is available on every floor for incoming calls. To ensure a supportive living environment, Resident Assistants reside on each wing to offer assistance and foster a positive community experience.

**Due to limited on-campus housing, priority in room allocations is given to international students**, with Kenyan students considered if space allows. All students who wish to live on campus are required to complete a Room Application Form. To be eligible, undergraduate students must be enrolled full-time with at least 9 credit units, while graduate students are required to take a minimum of 6 credit units in order to stay in the Residence Halls.

### Check-in / Check-out

When checking into the room, the resident will be required to fill in a check-in form with his/her Resident Assistant. This form will then be checked during checkout and any damages other than normal wear and tear will be the resident’s responsibility.

### Important Information

Secure and supportive living environment plays a critical role in students’ academic journey. To supplement the limited on-campus housing, the University works closely with select private hostels to provide student accommodation.
To promote safety, reliability, and convenience, the university has a framework of vetting, accrediting and formalizing student housing arrangements. This framework considers regulatory requirements, safety, cleanliness and management. To this end the university has signed nomination agreements with three hostels. This list will be updated as and when other hostels are accredited and nomination agreement signed.

# FAQ

### 1. Who can apply for on-campus accommodation?

All USIU-Africa applicants (students)

### 2. How soon can I receive accommodation after applying for it? And is there a waiting list?

It depends on whether or not you are a local or international applicant. International applicants get priority. Yes, there is a Waiting List

### 3. How can I go about applying for accommodation as student/faculty?

Students – Fill and submit a Room Application Form. This form is available online or can be picked from the Admissions Office

Faculty – apply through their respective Deans

### 4. How many students and faculty can USIU-Africa accommodate?

Students – 256

Faculty – 15

### 5. Does the Room Application expire?

Yes, it does – 1 year after the date of application

### 6. What happens if I still do not have a room after 1 year from the date of application?

Please reapply for a position.

### 7. What is the cost of accommodation on campus?

KES. 30,000

### 8. Are there single or double rooms?

All rooms are double (twin beds)

### 9. What facilities are provided in a room?

Bed, mattress, study chair & table, wardrobe, book shelf

### 10. What are the leisure facilities offered in the USIU-Africa hostels?

Two TV rooms, indoor games and board games

### 11. How is the security for students or faculty who stay on campus?

Excellent. Hostel entrances are staffed by hostel receptionists round the clock, seven days a week

### 12. If I am staying on campus, what are the numbers I can call in case of emergency?

Ext. 262, 202, 327, 279, 266 – all available 24 hours a day

### 13. Can I have guests over when staying on campus?

We do not allow guests who are not members of our community

### 14. How many guests are allowed at a time, and is there a curfew?

The is no specific maximum number of guests within reason. The curfew time is from 10.30 p.m. to 8.00 a.m.

### 15. Who cleans the rooms, and how often?

Room attendants clean each room once a week. All public areas are cleaned daily.

### 16. Am I required to pay for the campus meal plan?

The campus meal plan is optional.

### 17. Where can I do my laundry?

In the Laundry Cubicles provided for in the hostels, or in the University Laundry (for a fee)

### 18. Where is the housing office located on campus?

In the New Block Hostel, Ground Floor
