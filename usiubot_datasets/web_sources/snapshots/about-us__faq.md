# FAQ

### 1. What programs does USIU-Africa offer?

USIU-Africa offers undergraduate, master's, and doctoral degree programs across disciplines such as business, science and technology, international relations, communication, pharmacy, psychology, and health sciences. The university also offers short courses and executive education programs.

### 2. Is USIU-Africa internationally accredited?

Yes. USIU-Africa holds dual accreditation — by the **Commission for University Education (CUE)** in Kenya and the **WASC Senior College and University Commission (WSCUC)** in the United States. This means degrees from USIU are recognized both locally and internationally.

### 3. How diverse is the student body at USIU-Africa?

USIU-Africa has one of the most diverse student populations in Africa, with students from over **70 nationalities**. This diversity enriches the academic and social environment, creating a global campus experience.

### 4. What financial aid options are available?

Students can apply for **internal scholarships**, financial aid, or benefit from external partnerships like the **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program**. The university also offers work-study opportunities and flexible payment plans for eligible students.

### 5. Are there online programs at USIU-Africa?

Yes. USIU-Africa offers fully **online degree programs**, including select bachelor's and master's degrees. These programs are designed for flexibility while maintaining the same academic quality as on-campus programs.

### 6. Can international students apply, and what support is available?

Absolutely. USIU-Africa welcomes international students and provides support with visa processing, orientation, housing, and integration through the Office of International Students and Programs.

### 7. What facilities are available on campus?

The campus includes modern lecture halls, a fully equipped library, sports and fitness facilities (including a swimming pool and gym), medical clinic, residence halls, cafeteria, media labs, and a wireless internet network across campus. You can check out our virtual tour [here](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/virtual-tour).

### 8. Is there accommodation available for students?

Yes. On-campus hostels are available for students, especially first-year and international students. Rooms are typically shared and come with basic furnishings and access to common facilities.

### 9. What career support does USIU-Africa offer?

The university has a dedicated **Career Services Office** that assists students with internship placements, CV writing, job search strategies, and connections to employers through job fairs and industry partnerships.

### 10. What makes USIU-Africa unique compared to other universities in Kenya?

USIU-Africa stands out for its **dual accreditation**, **global outlook**, **diverse student body**, and emphasis on **student-centered learning**. It offers a strong mix of academic rigor, industry relevance, and personal development in a multicultural environment.
