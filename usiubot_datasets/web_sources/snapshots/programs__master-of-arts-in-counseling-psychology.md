- Overview (first paragraph): "The Master of Arts in Counseling Psychology program equips aspiring professionals with the knowledge, skills, values, and ethical understanding needed for effective counseling. It provides a strong foundation in psychological assessment, therapeutic interventions, and evidence-based counseling techniques while fostering cultural sensitivity and critical thinking for practice in diverse settings. Emphasizing mental wellness and professional integrity, the program prepares graduates to address the needs of individuals, families, and communities. With a focus on ethical, legal, and multicultural considerations, students are well-equipped to work in clinical, educational, corporate, and community-based environments."
