- Admission Requirements (verbatim):
  "The program admits students once each year, during the Spring semester. All applicants must meet the following requirements:

  Applicants must hold a bachelor’s degree in Communication, Journalism, Public Relations, Advertising, or a related field, with a minimum GPA of 3.0 or its equivalent. Candidates with at least three (3) years of professional experience in communications or a related field will also be considered.

  In addition to the program-specific requirements, applicants must satisfy the University-wide graduate admission requirements, which include:
  - Proof of completion of secondary/high school education, or a signed attestation on the USIU application form (or its equivalent), indicating the month and year of graduation.
  - Official transcripts for all post-secondary education undertaken, with degrees awarded and dates clearly indicated.
  - Submission of a two-page essay addressing the following questions:
    - What experiences led you to select your professional objectives?
    - What other two factors would you like considered in evaluating your application?
  - Two (2) letters of recommendation.

  Students without a Communication Studies background will be required to take the following survey courses to give them some grounding in the discipline. Such students are required to apply a semester in advance, Fall (September) semester of each year, so they fulfill this requirement and be ready to start the MA program with their cohort in January.

  REMEDIAL COURSES — 12 UNITS
  - JRN 3000 News reporting and writing — 3 Units
  - JRN 3004 Media Ethics — 3 Units
  - JRN 3020 Development Journalism — 3 Units
  - JRN 3911 Principles of Public Relations — 3 Units"
