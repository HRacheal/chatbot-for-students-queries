2. Data Science (online): the FAQ entry requirements (KCSE/KACE/IGCSE level) and the FAQ reference to a "Bachelor of Science in Data Science and Analytics" conflict with the Admission Requirements section, which requires a bachelor's degree.
3. MBA-Health Leadership and Management: its Learning Outcomes text is identical to MBA-Global Business Management.
4. Journalism: the "Key Features" section describes a film programme.
5. MBA (Online): FAQ questions 3 and 4 are duplicates.
6. Admission requirements are missing from the DBA, PhD in Information Systems and Technology, PhD in International Relations, and Psy D pages.
7. Dual accreditation (CUE and WASC) is stated on the Data Science, Information Security, Information Systems PhD, International Relations PhD, DBA, Psy D and MSc Management & Organizational Development pages. The MBA Online page cites CUE and WSCUC in its FAQ.
