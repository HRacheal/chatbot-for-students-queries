## Computer Requirements:
- Record: `HB-9.3`  
- Document: USIU-Africa Student Handbook (`Student-Handbook.pdf`)  
- Pages: 36  
- Section number: 9.3  
- Parent section: USIU-ONLINE  
- Chunks: 2  
- Status: draft / pending_verification  
- Flags: none  
- Issues: V07, V23

**Extracted content:**

> 1. A computer (desktop/laptop) that is less than 5 years old will work.
> 2. The computer user must have administrative rights for downloading and installing software applications and extensions.
> 3. A Webcam shall be required for interacting in course activities that require video feedback from students (such as Zoom), video test proctoring (such as Respondus Monitor), or other third-party tools.
> 4. Stable Internet connection, preferably connected to an access point using an Ethernet cable. Consider having a backup internet connection such as a 4 G-enabled phone or modem.
> 5. An updated Internet browser, such as Mozilla Firefox and Google Chrome is preferred.
> 6. Respondus Lockdown Browser and Monitor for taking proctored tests and exams.
> 7. Microsoft Office suite (includes Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint). Students have free access to install the suite on their computers. (Consult ICT helpdesk).
> 8. Updated Anti-virus software.
> 9. Learning applications will be availed on the USIU-Africa network based on course outline/syllabus requirements. Lecturers will provide further details for the respective courses.
